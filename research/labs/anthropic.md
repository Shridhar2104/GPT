# Anthropic

Founded in 2021 by Dario Amodei, Daniela Amodei and other former OpenAI researchers, including
several authors of GPT-3 and the scaling-laws paper. Anthropic's research focuses on alignment,
interpretability and safety alongside frontier models (Claude).

- Research: <https://www.anthropic.com/research>
- Interpretability: <https://transformer-circuits.pub/>
- Engineering blog: <https://www.anthropic.com/engineering>
- News & model releases: <https://www.anthropic.com/news>
